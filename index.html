About

Mobile engineer who owns the whole stack

I'm a Flutter developer from South Cotabato, Philippines. Most of my work has been building apps that have to hold up in the real world — marketplaces moving live orders, drivers on unreliable connections, merchants who need their point-of-sale to stay in sync. That has pushed me well past the UI layer into Cloud Functions, payment infrastructure, background services and third-party integrations.

At HeyJag I architected and built a three-app ecosystem — a customer super-app, a merchant dashboard and a driver operations platform — plus the serverless backend that ties them together: automated order assignment, POS synchronisation on 30-minute cycles, and Stripe Connect payouts. Before that I worked with agencies and clients in Sydney, Honduras and for the Philippine Department of Agriculture, including native platform-channel work in Swift and C++ for a cross-platform desktop monitoring tool.
